How we design

A complete solution in as few products as possible.

Every new tool is another login, another bill and another thing to train. We design the other way around.

Friction mapping

We find where work gets stuck.

Every handoff, re-entry and wait mapped across people and systems. Then we fix the costliest friction first.

MCP connections

Connect, don’t replace.

Model Context Protocol lets assistants like Claude or ChatGPT safely work with the tools you already have, with approvals you control.

Zero UI

No new app to learn.

Faceless interfaces: work happens in plain English inside the chat, email and tools your team already lives in.

Fewest products

Ask about Atlas

Straight answers

Questions we hear first.

How is pricing handled?

It all lives in your private proposal page: options, phases and costs tailored to your goals and budget, with pieces you can take on yourself to save. You see the full path before anything starts — no surprise invoices.

Do you work with churches and nonprofits?

Yes, it’s a big part of our work. Ministries and nonprofits get discounted rates for the same scope and the same standards everyone else gets.

Who owns what you build?

With Compass and Expedition, you do: accounts, code, keys and documentation. With Atlas, we steward the infrastructure while you get the product and a team behind it.

What if I don’t know which model I need?

That’s normal. Tell us the problem and we’ll recommend the lightest option that gets you there — even if that’s a few hours of advice.

Not sure where to start?

Start with the problem.

Tell us what’s tangled. We’ll reply within 1–2 business days with a straight answer on whether — and how — we can help.
